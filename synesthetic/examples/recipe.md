# Weeknight Lemon Garlic Chicken

Serves 4. Prep 15 minutes, cook 25 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lb chicken thighs, boneless
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced (about 3 tbsp)
- 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1 cup chicken broth

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 400 °F (200 °C).
2. Brown the chicken in the oil for 4 minutes per side.
3. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds. Do not let it burn.
4. Pour in the broth and lemon juice, then bake for 15 to 18 minutes.

Caution: the chicken must reach 165 °F inside before serving.

Per serving: about 320 calories and 28 g protein.

Tip: "Let it rest 5 minutes" so the juices settle. For a side dish, see the rice recipe on page 42.
