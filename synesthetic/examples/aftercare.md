# After Your Dental Visit

Visit date: Nov 3, 2026. Follow-up: Nov 17, 2026 at 2:30 PM.

## Pain relief

Take 1 tablet (400 mg ibuprofen) every 6 to 8 hours as needed, with food.

Do not take more than 1,200 mg in 24 hours unless your dentist tells you to.

## First 24 hours

- Bite gently on the gauze for 30 minutes.
- Do not rinse, spit or use a straw today.
- Keep your head raised when lying down.

## Days 2 to 7

Rinse gently with warm salt water (1/2 teaspoon in 8 oz of water) 3 times a day.

Eat soft foods. Avoid hot drinks for the first 48 hours.

## When to call

Warning: call right away if bleeding lasts more than 4 hours, or if you have a fever over 101 °F.
