# Cell Biology: Chapter 4 Review

## Key terms

Mitochondria: the organelles that turn glucose into usable energy (ATP).

Osmosis means water moving across a membrane toward the side with more dissolved solutes.

Homeostasis: the body keeping internal conditions steady, such as a temperature near 37 °C.

## Numbers to remember

- One glucose molecule yields about 30 to 32 ATP.
- The human body has roughly 37 trillion cells.
- A red blood cell lives about 120 days.

## Formulas

Surface area to volume: SA/V = 3/r for a sphere

Osmotic pressure: Π = iMRT

$$ΔG = ΔH − TΔS$$
