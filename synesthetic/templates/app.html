How it works

Synesthetic Layer reads a document and letters each kind of detail in its own color, so numbers like 1,250, dates like Aug 14, "quotes", page 5 references and do not warnings stand out the same way every time.

  1. 1. It reads your textIt finds numbers, dates and times, warnings, quotes, references, links and IDs. No tagging, no emoji.
  2. 2. Each kind gets its colorThe same kind of detail is always the same color, in every document. The colors are yours to change in Editor.
  3. 3. You pick the lookColored text, highlights, both at once, or a plain underline. Add the grammar layer, or switch to color-blind safe colors.
